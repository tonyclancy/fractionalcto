How I Help

Practical technology leadership, matched to where you are.

Every engagement starts with the same question: what does the business actually need from engineering right now? The answer sets the scope — a few days a month, a full-time interim seat, or a fixed assessment.

Engagement Models

Three ways to work together.

Ongoing

Fractional CTO

Part-time, embedded technical leadership — typically a set number of days per week or month — for companies that need senior judgment on architecture, hiring, and roadmap without a full-time executive.

Best for
A founder-led or growth-stage company that needs an experienced technical counterpart.
Working model
A recurring, embedded cadence with the executive team and engineering leaders.
First outcome
Shared priorities, decision ownership, and an executable near-term plan.
Full-time, temporary

Interim CTO

I step into the seat full-time during a leadership transition — protecting delivery, stabilizing the team, and supporting the search for a permanent hire, with a clean handoff at the end.

Best for
A company between CTOs, navigating a merger, or carrying immediate delivery risk.
Working model
Full executive ownership until the business is ready for a permanent leader.
First outcome
A continuity and risk plan covering people, roadmap, platform, and stakeholders.
Fixed scope

Technology & Engineering Assessment

A focused engagement for technical due diligence, architecture and delivery risk, or roadmap and organization alignment.

Best for
A CEO, board, or investor who needs an independent technical read before making a larger commitment.
Working model
Interviews, operating and architecture review, and direct working sessions.
Deliverable
An executive readout with prioritized risks, decisions, and a practical action plan.

The Process

What working together looks like.

01

Listen & Diagnose

Understand the business goals, the current team, and where technology is helping or getting in the way.

02

Set the Plan

A concrete roadmap, org structure, and architecture direction — sized to what the team can actually execute.

03

Lead & Build

Run the team, make the calls, remove delivery constraints, and turn the plan into shipped work.

04

Transition & Sustain

Hand off cleanly to a permanent leader or an operating rhythm the team can run without me.
