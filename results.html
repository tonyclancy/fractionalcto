CTO · Imagine Learning, formerly Edgenuity

Scale the organization and make the platform dependable.

Situation

A fast-growing learning platform needed substantially more capacity, reliability, delivery discipline, and engineering leadership.

Leadership

Grew the technology team from 25 to 100+ in six months, modernized infrastructure, and established automated testing and operating discipline.

Outcome

Increased concurrent-user capacity by more than 200%, raised uptime from 96% to 99.9%, and moved automated test coverage from near-zero to 75%+.

CTO · BookNook

Improve reliability while expanding product capability and trust.

Situation

The company needed a clearer product and engineering operating model, stronger platform reliability, and enterprise-ready security and compliance.

Leadership

Reorganized the team, aligned the roadmap, strengthened QA and automation, led SOC2 work, and delivered a patent-pending tutor-matching capability.

Outcome

Raised uptime from 99.7% to 99.95% in six months, achieved a 99% tutor-match rate, and progressed from SOC2 Type 1 to Type 2.

SVP Engineering · ConnectWise

Bring acquired teams into one engineering operating model.

Situation

A global software portfolio needed consistent leadership, governance, and execution across a large organization and multiple acquired businesses.

Leadership

Led 500+ people with full engineering budget ownership and established shared governance for architecture, scalability, and security.

Outcome

Integrated four acquired entities into a unified engineering model with clearer decision rights, operating standards, and executive visibility.

VP Development · CentralSquare Technologies

Lead through a three-way merger without losing operating control.

Situation

Three public-safety technology businesses were coming together across dozens of products, locations, and engineering teams.

Leadership

Led a 600-person organization across 51 products and six locations with an approximately $70M annual budget, while launching an applied machine-learning team.
