The people and science behind the journey

First Descent brings fictional worlds to life with original game work, open-source software, freely licensed fonts and credited astronomical observations.

Observation images are resized, WebP-encoded and softly blended into the starfield. Destinations, rotation and travel times are fictional. No endorsement by the credited organizations is implied.

Asteroid models and surfaces
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/eros-3d-model/
Eros and Bennu visualization models adapted for the game.

Asteroid reference models and surface maps: NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD).
Eros: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/eros-3d-model/
Bennu: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/bennu-3d-model/
Adapted for First Descent: quantized geometry, compressed texture encoding, small silhouette variations, material tint and game animation. NASA does not endorse this game. These visualization models are not presented as scientific reconstructions.
