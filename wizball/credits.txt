WIZBALL — BROWSER EDITION

Original Wizball (1987)
Design: Jon Hare and Chris Yates
Programming: Chris Yates
Graphics: Jon Hare
Music: Martin Galway
Original cover illustration: Bob Wakelin
Original game: Sensible Software

Retrospec remake (2007)
Programming: Graham Goring
Graphics: Trevor "Smila" Storey
Music and arrangements: Infamous (Chris Nunn)
Original Macintosh conversion: Peter Hull
Original Linux conversion: Scott Wightman

Recovered-source revival (2026): Craig Chandler and contributors
Source: https://github.com/craigchandler/wizball-remake
Browser port: built from recovered source, revision
12759a163929a8d4bceb11932a1dbf875f01f50a, with browser adaptations.
